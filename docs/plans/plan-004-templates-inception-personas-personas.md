## Context

Third sub-phase of `templates-inception`, under plan-001. Its inputs are the accepted brief (§4
consumers, §5 contributions) and the vision ("Value by Audience", vision review: two catalogs,
regulatory `governance` packs). It produces `docs/01_vision/04_personas.md`, structured like
WingFoil2-Benchmark's personas.

## Steps

1. (facilitator) Write one persona per audience of the vision, plus the AI agent that works under a
   composed configuration. For each persona: who, goal, pain today, needs from the packs, success,
   the purpose served, and from which WingFoil release the persona is real.
2. (facilitator) Write the adoption profiles: WingFoil's own user types, referred to by profile,
   mapped to the axes that matter to each. They are a dimension for the catalog and the presets,
   not users of this repository.
3. Commit by hand and show the document to the approver with the open questions.
4. Record the review answers in the document, then plan `active → done`.

## Handoff

- **Approver:** reviews the document and answers the open questions.
- **claude:** writes and corrects it.
- No Memory gate. The approver's acceptance is recorded in the Execution Notes.
